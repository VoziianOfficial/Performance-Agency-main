Overview

Cookies and similar technologies can be used by websites to remember information, maintain functionality, understand usage or support third-party services.

Not every form of browser storage is technically a cookie. For clarity, this policy also discusses local storage, session storage and similar browser-side technologies where they perform a comparable function.

Current website behaviour

The website currently uses limited browser storage to remember the cookie notice preference. Additional analytics or advertising technologies should be reflected in this policy if they are enabled later.

02

What Are Cookies?

A cookie is a small piece of information that a website can ask a browser to store on a user's device.

Cookies may be temporary and expire when a browsing session ends, or they may remain available for a longer period depending on their purpose and configuration.

Similar technologies, such as local storage and session storage, can also save limited information within the browser.

Essential Browser Storage

The website currently includes a compact cookie notice.

When you accept the notice, a value may be stored in your browser so that the notice does not need to appear repeatedly on future page visits.

If you close the notice without accepting it, a temporary session value may be used so that the notice remains dismissed during the current browsing session.

These values are used for interface behaviour and do not themselves contain the contact form information submitted to the website.

07

Managing Cookies & Storage

Most modern browsers provide settings that allow users to review, remove or restrict cookies and stored website information.

Depending on the browser, you may be able to:

  • delete stored cookies;
  • clear local website data;
  • remove site-specific storage;
  • block cookies for selected websites;
  • restrict third-party cookies; or
  • clear browsing data when the browser closes.

Removing the website's stored consent preference may cause the cookie notice to appear again on a later visit.

Restricting certain browser storage may also affect some website preferences or functionality.

08

Storage Duration

The length of time information remains available depends on the technology used.

Session storage normally remains available only for the current browser session, while local storage can remain available until it is removed by the website, the browser or the user.

Third-party cookies or storage, if any are enabled, may use different retention periods determined by the relevant provider and configuration.
