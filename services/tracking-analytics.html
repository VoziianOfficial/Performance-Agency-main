Tracking Review

What we check first.

We review whether important events are recorded consistently and whether obvious duplicates or missing conversion actions are affecting the account.
Not every tracked event should necessarily influence campaign optimization. The conversion set should reflect the actions that actually matter.
We examine whether CRM, qualified lead, offline conversion, purchase or revenue information can add more useful context after the first conversion.
The final measurement setup should support clearer campaign decisions rather than producing more dashboard numbers with no practical use.
