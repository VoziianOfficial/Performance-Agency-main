Automation Review

What should be automated?

Automation is most useful when the same reporting, checking or data-handling task happens repeatedly with predictable logic.
Automating incomplete or unreliable advertising data can simply make bad information move faster. Inputs have to be reviewed first.
Reports and alerts should surface information that someone can act on, not generate additional dashboards that are rarely used.
Automation can identify conditions and organize information, but strategic campaign decisions often still require business context.
