Privacy questions

We aim to handle personal information in a clear and proportionate way.

This policy applies to information submitted through the website, as well as limited technical information that may be generated when the website is accessed.

01

Overview

This website is operated for the purpose of presenting performance marketing services and allowing prospective clients to contact .

When you use the website, you may choose to provide information through the contact or audit request form. Certain technical information may also be processed by the website, hosting environment or other services required to operate the site.

No phone number required

The website does not request a phone number through its standard contact form.

02

Information We Collect

Information you provide

When you submit a contact or account review request, the information you provide may include:

  • your name;
  • company or organisation name;
  • business email address;
  • website address;
  • business type;
  • approximate monthly advertising budget;
  • the service or area you need help with; and
  • information included in your message or description of your business goal.

Technical information

Depending on the hosting environment and services configured for the website, limited technical information may be generated automatically when the website is accessed.

This may include information such as browser type, device type, referring page, requested pages, approximate access time, IP-related server logs and similar operational information.

The exact technical data available may depend on the hosting provider and any analytics or security services enabled for the website.

03

How We Use Information

Information submitted through the website may be used to:

  • review and respond to your enquiry;
  • understand your business and advertising requirements;
  • assess whether our services may be relevant to your request;
  • communicate about a requested account review, audit or potential engagement;
  • maintain website functionality and security;
  • identify technical problems or abuse; and
  • comply with applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

We do not need your contact information merely for you to browse the public pages of the website.

05

Sharing & Service Providers

Information may be processed by service providers where this is reasonably necessary to operate the website, deliver communications, maintain infrastructure or provide requested services.

Depending on the tools configured for the website, these providers may include categories such as:

  • website hosting providers;
  • email infrastructure providers;
  • website security or anti-abuse providers;
  • analytics providers, if analytics are enabled; and
  • other technical providers required to process a submitted request.

Personal information should not be sold simply because you submitted an enquiry through this website.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with a legal obligation, protect legitimate rights or respond to lawful requests.

06

Data Retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, maintaining business records and meeting applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on whether an enquiry progresses into a business relationship, whether records must be retained for legal or accounting purposes, and the nature of the information involved.

Enquiry data

If an enquiry does not progress into a business relationship, information should not be retained indefinitely without a legitimate reason.

07

Your Rights

Depending on the laws that apply to your situation, you may have rights concerning personal information held about you.

These rights may include the ability to request:

  • access to personal information;
  • correction of inaccurate information;
  • deletion of information in certain circumstances;
  • restriction of certain processing;
  • objection to certain processing;
  • portability of information where applicable; or
  • withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent.

These rights are not absolute and may depend on the applicable legal basis, exemptions and record-keeping obligations.
